Keep your archive from the browser

For an archive made from the archive template: deposit papers and new versions, give notices and new licences, withdraw, and curate collections. Each change is sent to the archive's repository as a request, which the archive's own workflow carries out with osheaf, exactly as from the command line.

Draft. OpenSheaf 0.1 is a draft specification: until version 1.0, a revision may change the format in incompatible ways.
