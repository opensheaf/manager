Keep your archive from the browser

For an archive made from the archive template: deposit papers and new versions, give notices and new licences, withdraw, and curate collections. Each change is carried out in this page by opensheaf-js, exactly as osheaf carries it out on the command line, signed with your keys, which your passkey unlocks, and committed to the archive's repository; or it is sent as a request to the archive's own workflow.

Draft. OpenSheaf 0.2 is a draft specification: until version 1.0, a revision may change the format in incompatible ways.
